A note from me

Dear both of you,

The things I mean, even when I forget to say them out loud.

✦

My dearest sisters,

Somehow, you have been my first friends, my fiercest teammates, and the people who can make me laugh when I am determined not to.

Thank you for every pep talk, every shared secret, every “don’t tell anyone” that absolutely stayed between us. Life feels lighter because I get to do it with you in my corner.

On this Raksha Bandhan, I hope you know that the thread is only a symbol. The real bond is in the everyday things — the check-ins, the memories, the ridiculous inside jokes, and the way we always find our way back to each other.

Keep being exactly who you are. I will be here for every version of you, for every new beginning, and for every cup of chai in between.

Love you endlessly,
Your favorite sibling ♡

My promise to you both:

More calls. More celebrations.
Always more us.
